# Drug Spending: Price vs. Volume

This project looks at how Medicare Part D prescription drug spending changed from 2020 to 2024. The main question is whether spending increased because drug prices went up or because more prescriptions were filled, and which of the two was the bigger driver of spending growth.

**Status:** In progress

## Data

The analysis uses the Medicare Part D Spending by Drug dataset published by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). The 2026 release covers data from 2020 through 2024 and reports total spending, dosage units, claims, and beneficiary counts for every drug in the program.

Source: https://data.cms.gov/summary-statistics-on-use-and-payments/medicare-medicaid-spending-by-drug/medicare-part-d-spending-by-drug
